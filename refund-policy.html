When you can get your money back, how to ask for it, and how long it takes to arrive.

The short version

Ask within the refund window and you get your money back, no reason needed.

Open: the refund window itself. Course pages currently advertise seven days. That figure has never been confirmed, and a refund promise is a contractual commitment — it needs deciding deliberately, then stating identically here, on every course page, and at checkout.

How to ask

Email hello@edbuddy.pro from the address you enrolled with and name the course. You don't have to give a reason. We'll confirm within one working day.

How the money comes back

Approved refunds go back to the method you paid with, through our payment processor. How fast it lands depends on your bank — usually five to seven working days for cards and UPI. GST charged on a refunded purchase is refunded with it.

A worked example

You paid ₹4,999 by UPI on the 3rd and ask for a refund on the 6th. We approve it the same day and instruct our processor. The ₹4,999, GST included, leaves us immediately and typically shows in your account within five to seven working days — the delay is your bank's, not ours. You keep nothing: course access ends when the refund is approved.

What isn't refundable

Some things, once used, can't be returned.

Open: whether a refund is available once a certificate has been issued, once most of a course is complete, or after a discounted or bundled purchase. Whatever is decided must appear at checkout before payment, not only here.

Still not clear?

Write to hello@edbuddy.pro and a person will answer — Monday to Saturday, 10am to 7pm IST.
