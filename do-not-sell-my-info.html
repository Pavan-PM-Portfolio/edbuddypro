We don't sell your data

edBuddy Pro does not sell personal data, and does not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. There is nothing here for you to opt out of.

What “sell” and “share” mean here

Under Californian law selling isn't limited to money changing hands — passing data to an advertising network in exchange for anything of value counts, and so does sharing it so someone can target you across other sites. We do neither. Our analytics tell us which lessons get used; they do not build a profile of you for anyone else.

Why this page exists

“Do Not Sell My Info” is a right created by the California Consumer Privacy Act and similar US state laws. It applies to businesses that meet certain revenue or data-volume thresholds and handle the data of California residents.

Open: a decision, not copy. If edBuddy Pro doesn't meet those thresholds, publishing this link implies a compliance programme that doesn't exist. Either remove the link, or confirm the thresholds are met and build the process behind it — verified requests, a 45-day response window, and a designated contact method.

Your rights, wherever you live

You can always ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@edbuddy.pro and we'll handle it under our Privacy Policy.
