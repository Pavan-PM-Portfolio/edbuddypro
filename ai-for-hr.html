AI for HR Professionals — Course Overview

edBuddy Pro's AI for HR course is a hands-on generative AI programme built for the people who actually run HR — hiring, onboarding, performance management and people data. Across 10 modules and 60 topics you move from AI foundations and prompt engineering for HR through AI-powered talent acquisition, L&D, performance automation, workforce analytics, no-code HR automation and HR AI agents — closing with AI governance, DPDP compliance and an implementation roadmap for your own function. No coding at any point.
