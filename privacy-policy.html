How edBuddy Pro collects, uses and protects your personal data, and the rights you have over it under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who we are

edBuddy Pro operates edbuddy.pro and the courses offered on it. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 we are the data fiduciary for the information described below. You can reach us at hello@edbuddy.pro.

Open: registered entity name, CIN, registered office, and the grievance officer's name and contact.

What we collect

Three kinds of data. What you give us — your name, email, phone number, the role you pick, and anything you type into a form or a lab. What your use of the courses tells us — the lessons you opened, how far through a module you are, the labs you finished, the device you used. And confirmation of payment from our processor, which includes the last four digits of a card and nothing more.

We never see or store a full card number.

What that looks like in practice

You enrol in AI for HR. We store your name and work email so we can issue the certificate in the right name, the fact you completed module 4, and that your payment succeeded. We do not store your card number, your employer's HR data, or anything you paste into a third-party AI tool during a lab — that goes to that tool, under its own terms.

Why we use it

To deliver the course you bought, record your progress, issue and verify your certificate, answer your questions, and send you service messages about your enrolment. Separately, to understand which parts of a course work and which do not, so we can fix the parts that don't.

Where we rely on your consent, such as marketing email, you can withdraw it at any time, and withdrawing is as easy as giving it.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with the service providers who run payments, email delivery, hosting and analytics on our behalf, under contract and on our instructions.

Open: the list of sub-processors, what each one receives, and where each stores it. This should be published and kept current.

How long we keep it

Account and progress data stays for as long as your account is active, so your certificate remains verifiable. Financial records are kept for the period Indian tax law requires.

Open: the retention period for each category, and what triggers deletion once it lapses.

Your rights

You can ask for a summary of the data we hold about you, ask us to correct or complete it, ask us to erase it where we no longer need it, and nominate someone to exercise these rights if you cannot. Write to hello@edbuddy.pro and we will answer within the statutory period. If our answer doesn't satisfy you, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

How to actually make a request

Email us from the address on your account with what you want — a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion. We may ask one question to confirm it's you. You'll get a written answer, and if we say no, we'll say why.

Deleting your account also deletes your certificate record, so it can no longer be verified by an employer. We'll warn you before that happens.

Children

edBuddy Pro is built for working professionals. We do not knowingly process a child's personal data, and we do not track or advertise to children. If you believe a child has given us data, tell us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that materially affects you, we will tell you by email or a notice on the site before it takes effect. The date at the top always shows the current version.

Still not clear?

Write to hello@edbuddy.pro and a person will answer — Monday to Saturday, 10am to 7pm IST.
